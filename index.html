Emotion is a complex reaction pattern involving physiological, behavioral, and experiential elements that humans experience in response to situations they find personally significant. It is a complex psychological phenomenon that includes a subjective experience, a physiological response (like changes in heart rate), and a behavioral or expressive response (like a facial expression or action). Emotions help us deal with matters, influence decisions, and provide information about our experiences.
